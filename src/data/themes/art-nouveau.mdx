Art Nouveau / Botanical uses flowing plant forms, fine curved borders, and serif headings. It suits cultural projects, botanical brands, editorial pages, and selected product collections. Ornament should remain subordinate to the content.

## Key Visual Elements

Use a small number of leaf and stem drawings with curved frames. Place gold accents on borders and decoration while keeping text dark green. Do not cover labels, buttons, or photographs with ornamental lines.

## Layout and Interaction

Reserve decorative framing for the hero and important content. Keep forms simple and readable. On small screens, reduce ornament and retain a clear document order. Use stable borders and color changes for interactive states.

## Typography

Cormorant Garamond provides expressive display headings. Lato keeps paragraphs and controls readable. Avoid using decorative serif lettering for small labels or long body text.
