Galaxy is a cosmic style for creative portfolios, brand pages, and entertainment products. Large display type and an asymmetric orbital illustration define the composition. A single background nebula supports the illustration without covering text.

## Layout

Place the main heading beside the orbital illustration. Use panel widths to show which content matters most. Choose sections from the actual work or product. Avoid adding statistics or testimonials to fill the layout.

## Color and type

Use coral for primary actions and lavender for links. Use pale gold sparingly in the orbital illustration. Keep forms and reading content on opaque plum panels. Use Syne for display headings and DM Sans for body text.

## Motion

Slow orbital rotation is optional. Keep the nebula static and leave text and controls stationary. Stop rotation for reduced motion. Do not use autoplay video or a particle field as a default.
