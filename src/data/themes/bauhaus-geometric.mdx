Bauhaus Geometric combines basic shapes, primary colors, and deliberate alignment. This catalog theme uses those visual elements for brand pages, portfolios, and editorial layouts. Shapes should support the hierarchy rather than fill every empty area.

## Key Visual Elements

Use a small number of circles, triangles, and rectangles. Align shapes with section boundaries and text columns. Keep the palette limited to red, blue, yellow, and neutral surfaces. Avoid scattered patterns that obscure the main content.

## Layout and Interaction

Use asymmetric sections with a clear reading order. Keep controls rectangular and easy to identify. Decorative geometry should not receive keyboard focus. On mobile, place large compositions below the main heading and action.

## Typography

Outfit gives headings a geometric form. Inter supports descriptions and controls. Use large headings selectively and keep body text at a comfortable reading width.
