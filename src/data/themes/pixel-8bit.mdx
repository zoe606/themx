Pixel design uses bitmap graphics, sharp edges, and a limited palette inspired by early game interfaces. It differs from Retro/Y2K through its pixel grid and stepped shapes, rather than metallic surfaces and futuristic display type.

## When to Use

This style suits game websites, personal portfolios, and playful educational pages. Avoid it for long reading pages and formal workflows that need neutral typography.

## Key Visual Elements

Use integer scaling for pixel graphics and keep edges sharp. Limit bitmap typography to short headings. Maintain readable body text, touch targets, and explicit status labels. Do not add rapid flashes or require animation to understand the page.

## Typography

Press Start 2P gives short headings a bitmap appearance. Space Mono is easier to read for descriptions and controls. Use generous line height and avoid long all-capital paragraphs.
