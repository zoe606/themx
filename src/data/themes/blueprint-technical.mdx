Blueprint design uses a technical grid, measured line drawings, and labels that connect a diagram to its subject. It suits engineering portfolios, technical education, and developer products with real diagrams or structured information.

## Key Visual Elements

Keep the grid lower in contrast than text and controls. Use cyan lines for diagrams, borders, and dimension markers. Decorative measurements should remain separate from application data and should not imply actual product specifications.

## Layout and Interaction

Align panels and annotations consistently. Give complex diagrams a readable description and enough space. On small screens, stack content and reduce decorative detail. Distinguish selected states and errors with labels as well as color.

## Typography

Space Mono supports short technical headings and labels. IBM Plex Sans keeps descriptions and forms readable. Use ordinary spacing for long paragraphs instead of monospace text throughout the page.
