Hand-drawn design adds handwritten headings, irregular lines, and small illustrations to a structured page. The uneven details should help distinguish the brand while keeping the reading order and controls clear.

## When to Use

This style suits educational pages, creative portfolios, and brand websites. Avoid it for dense financial or operational workflows that need precise compact alignment.

## Key Visual Elements

Use sketch details around headings, cards, and dividers. Keep body text in a readable font. Do not rotate forms or move buttons during interaction. Use illustrations with a defined purpose and keep decoration outside important text.

## Typography

Caveat works for short display headings. DM Sans keeps descriptions, form labels, and longer content readable. Avoid using handwriting for every paragraph.
